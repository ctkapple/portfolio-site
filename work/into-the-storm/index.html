The problem

What you set out to make and who it's for. What does this do that the deckbuilders you were playing didn't?

Process

§5 says lead with the playtest → identify friction → iterate loop. Pick two or three specific frictions that came out of playtests and walk each one through: what players actually did, what that revealed, what you changed, and whether the change worked. Showing a change that *didn't* work is worth more than three that did.
